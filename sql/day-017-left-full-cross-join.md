# 📅 Day 17 – RIGHT JOIN, FULL OUTER JOIN & CROSS JOIN

Today, I expanded my SQL JOIN knowledge by learning three additional JOIN types and understanding how different JOINs affect the results returned by a query.

I also discovered the importance of **compound JOIN conditions**, where multiple columns are used together to accurately match related records.

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

- RIGHT JOIN
- FULL OUTER JOIN
- CROSS JOIN
- Compound JOIN Conditions
- Matching Records Using Multiple Keys

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

- Learned that RIGHT JOIN preserves all rows from the right table.
- Understood how FULL OUTER JOIN combines all matching and non-matching records from both tables.
- Practiced generating every possible combination of records using CROSS JOIN.
- Learned how compound JOIN conditions improve data accuracy.
- Continued building confidence with multi-table SQL queries.

## 🧠 Reflection

Today's lesson helped me understand that choosing the correct JOIN is just as important as writing the query itself.

One of my biggest lessons was learning when a relationship requires more than one JOIN condition. Matching on multiple columns ensures reports remain accurate and prevents incorrect data from appearing in the results.
