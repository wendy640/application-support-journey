## 🧠 Reflection

Today's lesson taught me that writing JOINs becomes much easier when I focus on the relationships between tables instead of trying to memorize SQL syntax.

Before writing a JOIN, I now ask:

1. What information do I need?
2. Which table contains it?
3. How are those tables connected?

This simple framework has already made me much more confident working with relational databases.
